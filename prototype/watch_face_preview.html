Design Principles Applied

  • True Black Background — Every black pixel is OFF on OLED, saving battery
  • Catmull-Rom Smoothed Curve — Temperature line is mathematically smoothed, not jagged
  • Opacity-Based Clouds — Cloud density shown via transparency, not hatching
  • Step Goal Fill Effect — Time digits fill from bottom as you walk
  • Glanceable Design — Full 72-hour forecast readable in < 2 seconds
